Dexle

Swap the Pokémon until every link between neighbours is true.

Opening today's puzzle…

How to play

Six Pokémon, five links. Each link sits under two neighbours and has to be true for that pair, reading left to right. Click a Pokémon, then click another to swap them. Submit when you think every link holds. Wrong guesses cost a life, and each guess shows which links were right.
